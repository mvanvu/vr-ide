D49 (T9) Replace race window: renameat2(RENAME_EXCHANGE), compare the swapped-out file, swap back on mismatch. Still unprotected: a writer that holds the old file open and writes between our compare and the unlink, or a second write (e.g. a rename over the target) between our swap and the swap-back. Without EXCHANGE (non-Linux, EINVAL filesystems), a write between the re-read and rename(2) is lost (like D32). If the swap-back or the post-swap read fails, the swapped-out file is kept as `<name>.vr-ide-conflict-PID-N` and the error names it; never deleted.
